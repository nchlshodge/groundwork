Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Groundwork is a prayer practice app published by Nicholas Hodge ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Groundwork collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. We have tried to write it in plain language.

The short version
  • You sign in with Google. We save the prayer board and progress you create so it is there on every device.
  • Your prayer board is private to your account. We don't sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your sign-in is shared with our other apps, Scripture Scout and Rhythm. Deleting your account in one deletes it in all of them.
  • You can erase your Groundwork data or delete your account at any time in the app: Settings (⚙) → Privacy & data.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you tap "Continue with Google," Google shares your email address, name, profile photo link, and a unique account ID with us through our sign-in provider. We never see your Google password.

What you add to the app. This includes:

  • Prayer board entries in each section (praise, worship, petition, intercession, confession, forgiveness, testimonies), including the names of people you pray for and any notes you write about them or about answered prayers.
  • Your prayer history: which prayer model you completed and when, used for your streak and weekly goal.
  • Your lesson progress.
  • Fasting details: the type of fast, an optional intention you write, and when it started.
  • Your settings: theme, weekly goal, reminder schedule preference, and your last-used prayer model.

Prayer requests can be personal, and may include information about other people. Please only enter what you are comfortable storing in the app.

Information stored on your device. Your browser's local storage keeps your sign-in session, a list of changes waiting to sync if you are offline, and small preferences such as which encouragement message to suggest next for each person you pray for. This stays on your device until you erase your data, delete your account, or clear your browser data.

Technical information. Like any website, the services that host and deliver Groundwork may log technical details such as your IP address and browser type when your device connects.

What we do not collect. We do not use analytics or advertising tools, and we do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. The "let them know I prayed" feature opens your own email or messaging app with a draft message. We don't send that message, and we never see or store the email address or phone number you type.

2. How we use your information

We use your information only to run Groundwork: to sign you in, to save and show your prayer board, and to calculate your streaks and progress. We do not sell your information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use it to build profiles about you.

3. Who can see your information

In the app, only you. Database rules limit each account to its own rows, so other users of Groundwork, Scripture Scout, or Rhythm cannot read your prayer board.

Legal requests. We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so.

Links to other sites. Groundwork links to Google Calendar (when you add a prayer-time reminder, which pre-fills a generic event title) and to book pages on Amazon. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

4. One account across our apps

Groundwork uses the same sign-in as our other apps, Scripture Scout and Rhythm. Each app stores its own data separately, but they share your account. Because the account is shared, deleting your account from any of these apps deletes the account and data in all of them. If you only want to remove your Groundwork data, use "Erase my Groundwork data" instead.

5. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Download a copy. On the Board tab, tap "Download a backup copy" to save your prayer board as a text file.
  • Erase your Groundwork data. Settings (⚙) → Privacy & data → "Erase my Groundwork data." This permanently removes your prayer board, prayer history, progress, fasts, and settings from Groundwork and keeps your account.
  • Delete your account. Settings (⚙) → Privacy & data → "Delete my account." This permanently deletes your account and your data in Groundwork, Scripture Scout, and Rhythm.
  • Ask us. You can also email us at the address below and we will delete your data or answer questions about it.

Deleted information may remain in routine database backups kept by our provider for a limited time before being overwritten. Data stored in your own browser can be cleared through your browser's settings.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists, so your prayer board is there when you return. When you erase your data or delete your account, it is removed as described above.

7. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access rules ensure each account can only reach its own data. No online service can guarantee perfect security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Children

Groundwork is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain uses of it. You can use the in-app tools above, or contact us to make a request. We do not sell personal information.

10. Where your information is processed

Our database and sign-in services run in the United States. If you use Groundwork from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
